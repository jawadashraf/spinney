# Phone calls in Spinney: staff guide

For liaisons, managers and admins.

You can now ring people straight from Spinney, and Spinney keeps a record of every call for you.

---

## Before you start (once only)

Ask your manager or admin to check that:

1. **You have a Spinney account** with the right role (Liaison, Volunteer liaison, Manager or Admin).
2. **Your phone extension is linked to your Spinney account.** Your extension is your phone number on the office system, for example *1000*.
3. **You have your Linkus login.** Linkus is the phone app you use on your computer.

Then, on your computer:

1. Open **Google Chrome**.
2. Add the **"Yeastar Linkus for Google"** extension from the Chrome Web Store. Ask for help if you haven't done this before.
3. Click the small **Yeastar icon** at the top right of Chrome and choose **Linkus Web Client**.
4. **Log in** with your Linkus details.
5. Plug in your **headset**.

---

## Every day

**Keep the Linkus tab open and logged in** while you work. Your calls go through it.

---

## Making a call

1. In Spinney, go to **Liaison → Calls → Due today**.
2. Open the call you want to make.
3. **Move your mouse over the phone number.** A small phone icon appears.
4. **Click the icon.** Linkus rings the number.
5. Talk as normal, then hang up.

You can also ring from:
- a service user's page (the number is just under their name),
- the **Service Users** list (the Phone column),
- the **People** and **Companies** lists.

> You can't ring a number while it's inside a box you're editing. Use the number shown under the person's name instead.

---

## After the call

**You don't need to record the call yourself.** Spinney does it for you within about a minute.

### If they answered

1. Go to **Liaison → Calls**. Your call now says **"Answered – add notes"**.
2. Open it and click **Add call notes**. **Answered** is already chosen.
3. Write your **call notes**.
4. Check the **next call date**. It's filled in for you from their call plan.
5. If you're worried about them, switch on **Raise support / safeguarding concern**.
6. Click **Save outcome**.

**Please add your notes the same day.** The call isn't finished until you do.

### If they didn't answer

Nothing to do. Spinney records the attempt and moves the call to the **next working day**.

If they still haven't answered after the last attempt, Spinney tells your manager.

### If you hung up quickly

If you rang off within **10 seconds** (for example, you dialled the wrong number), it doesn't count as an attempt.

---

## When someone rings you

- **If Spinney knows the number,** Linkus shows their name and can open their page in Spinney. If they have a call due today, that call opens, ready for your notes.
- **If Spinney doesn't know the number,** see *Calls from unknown numbers* below.

---

## Seeing past calls

Open a service user, person or company and click the **Phone calls** tab. You'll see:
- when each call happened, and who made or took it,
- whether it was answered, and how long it lasted,
- **Play**, to download the recording.

---

## Calls from unknown numbers

Calls with numbers that aren't on anyone's record appear under **Liaison → Unmatched calls**. You'll see the calls you handled. Managers and admins see all of them.

For each call, choose one of these:

| If the caller is… | Do this |
|---|---|
| **Someone already in Spinney** | Click **Link to contact**, find them, and leave **"Save this number…"** on. Their calls will be recognised from now on. |
| **A new person needing help** | Click **…** then **Start enquiry**. The number and time are filled in for you. |
| **Spam or a wrong number** | Click **…** then **Dismiss**. |

Calls nobody deals with are deleted automatically after **90 days**.

---

## Recordings

- Calls are recorded. **Play** downloads the recording to your computer.
- Recordings are personal information. **Don't share them**, and delete the downloaded file when you've finished.

---

## Something not working?

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| No phone icon on the number | Refresh the page (press **F5**, or **Cmd+R** on a Mac). Check that the Linkus tab is open and logged in. |
| Still no icon | The number may be wrong or incomplete. A UK mobile starts with **07** and has 11 digits. Fix it on their record. |
| My call isn't in Spinney | Wait a minute and refresh. If it's still missing, tell your manager (your extension may not be linked). |
| A known person's call is under Unmatched calls | Their number is missing or different on their record. Use **Link to contact**. |
| The wrong call was updated, or none was | Tell your manager. A call due on a later day isn't updated early. |

## For managers and admins

### Adding a new member of staff

1. In Spinney, go to **Workspace → Users**, open the person, and give them a **Liaison**, **Volunteer liaison**, **Manager** or **Admin** role.
2. In the **Yeastar phone system**, link their **extension** to their Spinney account: **Integrations → CRM → Spinney → User Association**. Choose their name next to their extension, then **Save**. If you don't have access to the phone system settings, ask whoever manages it.
3. Make sure they have a **Linkus login**.
4. Ask them to ring a mobile and answer it. The call should appear in Spinney within a minute.

**Until step 2 is done, none of that person's calls are recorded in Spinney.**

### Keeping things tidy

- Check **Liaison → Unmatched calls** regularly. The number next to the menu shows how many are waiting.
- **Dismiss** spam in bulk: tick the calls, then click **Dismiss selected**.
- Keep an eye on calls marked **"Answered – add notes"** that are more than a day old, and remind liaisons to finish them.
- When someone leaves, remove the link between their extension and their Spinney account on the phone system.
